# M3 method and software sources

Author: Yang Qi.

These references are supplied in the owner package's reference/EXTERNAL_SOURCES.json; no external retrieval, package installation or performance verification was performed during M3.

- Schulman et al. (2017), [Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347): clipped policy surrogate and finite reuse of rollout batches.
- Schulman et al. (2015), [High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438): TD residuals and generalized advantage estimation.
- [TRL PPO Trainer documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/ppo_trainer): conceptual role comparison only. TRL is not installed or used by this native PyTorch implementation.
- Existing local Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, Transformers, PEFT and PyTorch provide the frozen model/tokenizer, native generation, LoRA and tensor/optimizer software. Their exact versions, identities and installed metadata are bound in the run artifacts.

The pure PPO arithmetic and its 28 tests originate from the supplied M3 package. Local integration and reports retain Yang Qi as sole project author. Method/software citations do not imply coauthorship or exact replication of every framework default.
